How it works

No app, no wearable, no test to take. Just a FaceTime call.

1

Grandpa calls Remi

Whenever he likes, on FaceTime. Remi shows family photos and asks the questions you sent.

2

Remi chats and remembers

A warm 5–10 minute chat about his life. Next time, Remi picks up where they left off.

3

You get his stories

A short summary and fun facts after every call, plus a gentle note if it may be time to check in.

What Remi gently keeps an eye on

Four signals from speech research. Each one is compared only with Hal's own usual, never with anyone else.

Pauses & hesitationStopping mid-thought to find a word.

Speaking paceHow quickly he talks.

Vague words"That thing" in place of a name.

Retold storiesThe same story several times in a week.

His own usualMany things can cause a changeA note, never a verdict

He's in charge of what's shared

  • Grandpa says yes on his first call
  • You see summaries and fun facts, never transcripts
  • Audio is deleted right after each call

A nudge, not a diagnosis

Remi isn't a medical device and doesn't diagnose anything. It notices changes compared with his own usual, which can have many causes, and suggests talking with his doctor.
